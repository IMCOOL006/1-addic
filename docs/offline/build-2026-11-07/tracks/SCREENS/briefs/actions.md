## Rules that bite
SOS is never gated; no screen here covers or delays the SOS button, and urgent help stays one tap away (free-text fields may call E.crisisKeywords(text) when present and offer #/sos/urgent). Nothing decreases: removing a plan removes no event; XP comes from events only. No streaks, no random reward, no points for claimed sober days, no overdue/late/missed wording. Original words, person-first, never "addict" as a noun, grade 6-8, adult tone; short copy; where wording touches substance use or crisis, mark it clinicalReview and write the shorter version. Practice scripts are the content lane's. 44 px targets, labels, visible focus; no animation when settings.reducedMotion or calm. No network, no trackers; read-aloud only via UI.speak (O4). Locale 'en' (O5).
