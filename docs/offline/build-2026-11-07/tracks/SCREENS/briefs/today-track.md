## 1. What the lane is for
Three lines. Today is the first screen after onboarding: it greets, offers the right check-in, shows the plan, the learning focus, quests and the opt-in counters, never moralising and never decreasing. Track is where the person logs a craving, feeling, check-in, activity or slip in a few taps, reads the timeline and (tier 2) the Insights cards. The Harbor scene (tier 2) is the layered SVG whose light only ever grows.
