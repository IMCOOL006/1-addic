## 7. R-O-6 additions (2026-11-08 03:03 IST): the quest-line, the daily objectives, the run

The owner's R-O-6 (03:03, recorded in `outbox-OWNER.md` with the owner's words) adds three features to V3, tier 1 by the owner's "add", under the house rules (BUILD-PLAN.md section 2: nothing derived decreases; no random rewards; no points for claimed sober days; SOS, help, lessons, Settings and the person's data never gated; original words; person-first). The owner's window's shaping in R-O-6 is the default; the questions V-Q9 to V-Q13 refine it.

**The three features, in the app's terms.**
- **The quest-line ("Your way"):** a spine of 8 to 12 quests in a fixed order, each with a condition over existing events (the first log, the first SOS drill, the M0 lesson, a Battle Plan, a Safety Net contact, three skill uses, a Weekly Review, ...) and a deterministic reward: a feature id from `Content.features` that unlocks early (a tool or mini-game, a game surface's extra, a Harbor scene or theme, the Explorer), never SOS, help, a lesson, Settings, export or the person's data. `Engine.unlocked(view)` is the union of what the modules reveal today (`Engine.revealed`) and what the quest-line rewarded: a superset, nothing ever re-locks. A completed step is claimed by one tap (the reward sheet names what opened) or shows as claimed when the person next opens Today.
- **The daily objectives:** three small objectives a day (two drawn deterministically from a pool by the date and the person's state, one the person may swap once a day), each done by a tap or automatically by its matching event; an objective day (at least one done) adds one unit of growth to Your Harbor (a visible element that stays: a lantern along the pier, a tree on the hill, a boat at anchor), never removed; a missed day pauses growth, it never undoes it.
- **The run ("days shown up"):** the current run, the best run and the total of days shown up, where a day shown up is any day with a log, a lesson, a practice, an objective or an SOS drill (never a claimed sober day); the total and the best never decrease; one grace day a week keeps the run and shows as "kept"; a break starts a new run with a "you came back" line and nothing earned is lost; no shame line, no push, no countdown. It sits in Today's header line and in Your Harbor's "Where you stand". The counters of the sober days (opt-in, O6) stay separate and unchanged.
