## 8. Safety and content rules
SOS is never gated; Settings, Workbook and Plan never block it, and a locked notebook still offers Urgent help and SOBER. No network, tracker or remote font; contacts and all data stay on the device and leave only in the backup file the person saves. Nothing decreases: edits append responses, contacts are hidden not deleted, sample erase touches only marked rows. Original words only; person-first, adult-to-adult, grade 6-8, never "addict" as a noun; substance wording brief and clinical (no how to get, prepare or take); any crisis or medical line you write carries a clinicalReview comment and numbers carry verify. Accessible: labels, visible focus, 44 px targets, reduced motion, contrast, keyboard.
