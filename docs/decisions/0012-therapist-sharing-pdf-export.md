# ADR 0012: Sharing with a therapist or sponsor — PDF export

- **Decision:** D12 (PLAN §15) · **Status:** Accepted (default) · **Date:** 16 October 2026

## Decision

A readable PDF export of My Recovery Plan and logs that the person shares themselves. No live sharing in version 1.

## Consequences

- No server or consent flows for clinicians; built at Step 3.8.
