# ADR 0018: Learning pace — Steady by default, plus Custom and Free

- **Decision:** D18 (PLAN §15) · **Status:** Accepted (default, extended) · **Date:** 16 October 2026

## Context

The plan offered Gentle, Steady and Focused. The owner wants a custom option and a no-limit option as well.

## Decision

Steady (one module a week) is the default. Gentle (one every two weeks) and Focused (two a week) remain. Two more options: **Custom** (the person sets their own number of modules per week) and **Free** (no weekly targets at all: the roadmap shows only the recommended order and the person's progress, with no dates). Changeable any time.

## Consequences

- Onboarding step 7 offers all five; the Roadmap's Plan view handles Custom and shows no dates in Free; the "this week's focus" card adapts or hides.
- PLAN §5.1 and §5.11 and Step 2.4 are updated.
