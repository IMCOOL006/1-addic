# ADR 0006: Game theme — the Harbor world with Two Brains mechanics

- **Decision:** D6 (PLAN §15) · **Status:** Accepted (default) · **Date:** 16 October 2026

## Context

PLAN §6.2 compared Two Brains, the Road, the Garden and the Harbor (reviewed in `docs/design/game-concept-review.md`).

## Decision

Two Brains mechanics (the companion, the Courtroom, the Distortion Sort, the six skill regions, event-sourced progression) in the Harbor world: Lighthouse, Cove, Garden, Pier, Compass Rose and Weather Station; cravings as passing weather; skills as anchors; SOS as safe harbor. Gentle visuals, on by default, with Calm mode one tap away. The Brain Map is the fallback if the harbor tests badly with people in recovery.

## Consequences

- Four conditions apply: nothing ever starts damaged, decays or is taken away; no storm visuals inside a live SOS session or the crisis screen; weather is never a dare; reactions are tested with people in recovery, including anyone for whom water or sailing is a hard topic.
- Step 0.5 writes the style guide for the harbor; Step 4.1 builds the map; Step 4.4 makes it a living scene.
