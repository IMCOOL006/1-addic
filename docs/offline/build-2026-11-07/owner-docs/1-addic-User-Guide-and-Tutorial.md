> [!SUMMARY]
> **1-addic is one file that you open in your phone's browser.** It helps you learn recovery skills, use them when a craving hits, and see what helps you over time. It works with no internet and no account. Nothing you type leaves your phone unless you save a backup file yourself. **SOS is always one tap away**, even before you set anything up and even when the app is locked. This guide walks you through the app in the order you will meet it. **One habit to learn today:** save a backup from Settings → Your data once a week.

## How to use this guide

This guide is for a person in recovery who opens the app on their phone, for the owner who checks it, and for the testers who walk through it. It reads as a journey, not a list of features. Chapter 1 is for a hard moment right now. Chapters 2 and 3 are your first hour and your first day. Chapters 4 to 9 follow the app's parts as you grow into them. Chapter 10 covers your data and settings. Chapter 11 says what the app will never do. Chapter 12 says what comes next. The last page is a Quick card with the five things to remember.

Each step tells you **what you see**, **what to tap**, **what happens**, and gives **one tip**. Every chapter ends with a **Try it** box, so you can learn by doing. (Screenshots come with the next edition.) Anything marked **coming** is not in this edition yet; the app says so on screen too, and never shows a button that does nothing.

The app uses a few words of its own.

| Word | What it means in 1-addic |
|---|---|
| Craving | A strong urge to use. The app treats it as a wave: it rises, peaks, and passes. |
| Slip | A time you used after deciding not to. It takes nothing away from what you have built. |
| SOS | The help button on every screen: a craving, a hard feeling, a slip, or urgent help. |
| Battle Plan | A plan made ahead for one risky time: what you will do, two backups, who to reach, how to leave. |
| Safety Net | A person you can call or text on a hard day. |
| Reasons Card | Your own reasons for this change, in your own words. SOS shows them to you. |
| Check-in | A one-minute look at your day, morning or evening. |
| Weekly Review | Five short screens, once a week: look back, then plan the week ahead. |
| The Harbor | A small harbor scene whose lights grow as you use your skills. It never dims. |
| Notebook | What the app calls your data. In discreet mode it is also the app's name. |

> [!WARNING]
> **1-addic is a skills notebook, not medical care.** It does not diagnose or treat, and it does not replace a doctor, a counsellor or a support group. If you are in danger, or you might hurt yourself or someone else, open SOS and tap **Urgent help**, or call your local emergency number now.

# 1. If you need help right now

## SOS in one tap

**What you see.** A round **SOS** button sits on every screen. In discreet mode it says **Help**. It is there before you set anything up, and on the lock screen too.

**What to tap.** Tap **SOS**.

**What happens.** The SOS screen opens with a big **Urgent help** button at the top, then four choices: **I'm craving**, **A hard feeling**, **I slipped** and **Leave SOS**. Once you have confirmed My Recovery Plan, a **My plan** card sits here too. Inside SOS there are no tips, tours or pop-ups, and the app never locks itself while you are here. After 8 pm the SOS screens use the dark colours, unless you chose the light theme.

**Tip.** You do not need a signal. SOS works with the phone in flight mode.

## Urgent help

**What you see.** A list of numbers, each one a tap to call. For India: the emergency number, an ambulance line, two mental-health helplines, a de-addiction helpline, a free counselling line and a poison information line, then links to find Alcoholics Anonymous, Narcotics Anonymous and SMART Recovery meetings. Under **Elsewhere**: two helpline directories and **Your local emergency number**. Each line has a **Copy** button. Below are two short cards: what to do if someone may have overdosed, and a note on stopping heavy daily drinking or sleeping pills safely.

**What to tap.** Tap a line to call it, or **Copy** to paste the number into another app.

**What happens.** Your phone's dialler opens. The app never makes the call itself and never records it.

> [!DANGER]
> **Every number in this edition is marked "please verify".** The list was collected from public sources and has not yet been checked by the owner. Check a number yourself before you rely on it. If a number does not connect, call your local emergency number (in India, 112).

## The lock screen

If you set a PIN and the app has locked, you still get help without it. The lock screen shows **Urgent help** first and **Ride the wave (SOBER)** second; in discreet mode they read **Help** and **Breathe**. SOBER is a two-minute breathing practice in five steps. Nothing you wrote shows on the lock screen; the help does.

> [!TIP]
> **Try it.** Open the app and tap **SOS**. Tap **Urgent help** and read the list. Pick one number and check it against a source you trust. Tap **Back to SOS**, then **Leave SOS**. You have now seen the one path you most need, and nothing was saved.

# 2. Opening the file the first time

## One file, no internet

1-addic is a single file called `1-addic.html`. You open it in a browser such as Chrome. It looks and works like an app, but nothing is installed and nothing is sent anywhere. The file carries a rule that makes the browser refuse every network request, so it cannot send your words out even by mistake. There is no account and no company server. Your notes live inside that browser, on that device.

## Putting it on a phone

1. Copy `1-addic.html` to the phone's **Download** folder (a USB cable, a memory card or a file share you trust).
2. Open the **Files** or **My Files** app, find `1-addic.html` and tap it. If asked, choose **Chrome**.
3. If Files will not open it, open Chrome and type `file:///sdcard/Download/1-addic.html` in the address bar. Chrome remembers it in its history.

On a computer, double-click the file or drag it onto a Chrome or Edge window.

**Tip.** Always open the file from the **same place, in the same browser**. The browser keeps your data for that one file. A copy in another folder, another browser or a private window starts empty.

## The saved label

The top bar always tells you where your data is.

| The label says | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Saved on this device · time | Your notebook is kept in the browser's storage and will be there next time. | Nothing, apart from a weekly backup. |
| Saved (fallback) | The main storage was not available, so a simpler one is holding your data. | Save a backup file now and then. Open the file in a normal Chrome window when you can. |
| Not saved — export before closing | The browser is blocking storage (often a private window). Your data lives only in memory. | Save a backup before you close the tab, then open the file in a normal window and bring it back. |

## Setting up: eight short steps

The first time you open the file, a welcome walks you through eight steps. **Next** keeps your answers; **Skip** drops that step's answers. Only the safety check cannot be skipped.

| Step | What you see | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Welcome | An early-access notice with **Got it**, and **Look around with sample data**. | Read it. To only look around, load the sample (below). |
| 2. Your notebook stays here | Two PIN fields, **Set PIN**, a **Discreet mode** box. | A PIN is recommended, never required: four to twelve digits. |
| 3. Your focus | What you want to change, the word that fits you (sober, clean, substance-free, in recovery), a start date, **I am not sure yet**. | Pick what fits. All of it can change later. |
| 4. Where you are | Five stages of change and a 0 to 10 slider. | There is no wrong answer. |
| 5. A quick safety check | Three boxes: heavy daily drinking, sleeping pills, opioids. A safety card appears if you tick one. | Read the card, tap **I understand**. |
| 6. Make SOS yours | Three reasons, one Safety Net (name and phone, optional), your region for crisis lines. | Three short reasons in your own words are enough. |
| 7. Your rhythm | Check-in times, your review day, your learning pace. | The defaults are fine to start. |
| 8. You are set | **Take the tour**, **Practise SOS once**, **Go to Today**. | Take the two-minute tour first if you can. |

**What happens.** Your answers become your profile. The three reasons go straight onto your Reasons Card and the Safety Net into your Support Network. If you set a PIN, every row of your notebook is encrypted on the device and the app locks after five minutes away.

> [!NOTE]
> **A forgotten PIN cannot be recovered.** That is what keeps your notebook private. The only way back is to erase the notebook on this device and bring back a backup file. SOS and urgent help work the whole time.

## Looking around with sample data

Tap **Look around with sample data** on the first step. The app fills itself with about four weeks of a made-up person's notebook: logs, check-ins, a few plans, two to-dos and four invented contacts with no phone numbers. Every row is marked **Sample**, none of it counts toward your own progress, and you can erase it in one tap from Settings → Your data.

> [!TIP]
> **Try it.** Open the file, tap **Look around with sample data**, tap **I understand**, and spend five minutes tapping through Today, Learn, Workbook, Track and Actions. Then go to **Settings → Your data → Erase everything**, confirm, and tap **Start again** to set the app up for real.

# 3. Your first day

## Today

**What you see.** A greeting, then two buttons: **Log a craving** and **SOS**. Below them, a stack of calm cards that change with the time of day and with what you have done: **Morning check-in** before noon or **Evening check-in** from 5 pm (or around the times you chose); **Today's plan**; **This week's focus** with **Continue learning**; **From your Reasons Card**, a different reason each day; **Your days** if you switched the counters on; **Momentum** (how many of the last 14 days you did something for your recovery); **Your progress**; **Explorer**; **A tip for today**; **More tools unlock as you learn**; **Your Harbor** at the end; and **Back up your notebook** when a backup is due.

**Tip.** Calm mode (Settings → Look) hides the Harbor, the progress panel and the rings, and keeps the rest.

## The quick log

**What you see.** Tap **Log a craving** on Today, or **+ Log** on Track. On Track you first choose: **Log a craving**, **Log a feeling**, **Check-in**, **Log an activity** or **Note a slip**.

**What to tap.** For a craving: slide **How strong was it?** from 0 to 10, tap the chips under **What set it off?**, and, if you like, write what went through your mind. Tick **It was a red-flag thought** if the thought pulled you toward using. Tap the skills you tried and the ones that helped. Tap **Save**.

**What happens.** A small note says **Craving saved.** and the new line sits at the top of **Your log**. Every honest log adds a little XP, and the skills you tapped are credited to your Harbor. A strength and one chip are enough.

**Tip.** The triggers you name in the **My triggers** exercise appear here as chips.

## The check-in

**What you see.** The morning check-in asks for your mood, your worry or stress, and one thing you want from today. The evening check-in also asks for your strongest craving today, your good moments, whether you used today, your sleep, and whether you went to a meeting or group.

**What to tap.** Move the sliders, answer **Yes** or **No**, tap **Save**.

**What happens.** The check-in joins your log. Seven evening check-ins complete the **7-Day Activity Experiment** quest, and after about a week Insights start to show what your days and your mood have to do with each other.

## The Harbor

**What you see.** A small harbor at the bottom of Today: a Lighthouse, a Cove, a Garden on the hill, a Pier with lanterns, a Compass Rose and a Weather Station. Each place stands for a kind of skill: thinking clearly, calm, joy, people, motivation and self-awareness.

**What happens.** A place grows brighter as you use its skills, from level 0 to 5. The light comes from everything you have ever logged, so it only ever grows. A slip dims nothing. The water drifts and the beam sweeps unless you turned motion off.

> [!TIP]
> **Try it.** On Today, tap **Log a craving**. Set the strength to 3, tap one chip, tap **Save**. Find the new line on Track, then go back to Today and look at **Momentum**. Tonight, do the evening check-in from the card on Today.

# 4. When a craving hits

## The craving flow

**What you see.** Tap **SOS**, then **I'm craving**. A 15-minute timer starts at the top. The first screen asks **How strong is it?** with buttons 0 to 10 and **Skip**.

**What to tap.** A number, or **Skip**. Then **Pick a tool**:

- **Ride the wave**: the SOBER steps, one at a time, with **Read aloud** in your device's own voice.
- **Remind me why**: your Reasons Card, one reason at a time, with **Another one**.
- **Call someone**: your Safety Nets, each with **Call** and **Text**, and a **Meetings** list.
- **Distract me**: your distraction list, or the app's starter ideas.
- **Talk back**: your own comebacks to red-flag thoughts, or the app's starter lines.

Or tap **Guide me** and the app runs three tools for you: the wave first, then the two that have helped you most. If you are somewhere risky, tap **I am somewhere risky**; leaving is a valid move.

**What happens.** After each tool the app asks **Did that help?** with **It helped** and **Not really**. Your answer teaches it which tools to offer first next time. When you are ready, tap **Check in with the craving**.

## The delay loop and the two outcomes

**What you see.** **How strong is it now?** with the 0 to 10 buttons, then **It passed** and **Still strong: wait 15 more**.

**What to tap.** If it is still strong, tap **Still strong: wait 15 more**: the timer starts again and you are back at the tools, as many times as you need. When it has passed, tap **It passed**.

**What happens.** The app asks **Did you use?** Either answer is OK here. **No** ends with **Wave surfed.** and a **Finish** button back to Today. **Yes** takes you gently into the slip flow. Either way one SOS session goes into your log, the skills you used are credited, and the places in your Harbor that hold those skills grow a little. Nothing you had before goes down.

## A hard feeling

Tap **SOS**, then **A hard feeling**, and pick the closest one: angry, sad or low, anxious or stressed, lonely, bored or restless, guilty or ashamed, hopeless, or something else. Anger gets **Give it a beat**: step away if you can and count to ten with the app, then a few things to say to yourself, then words you can use, then **Call someone**. The others get the SOBER steps, kind words to say to yourself, **One small good thing**, then **Call someone**. At the end the app asks **What helped?** and how strong the feeling is now. If you pick **Hopeless**, or type words that sound like danger, the urgent numbers appear at once and the app says little else.

## I slipped

Tap **SOS**, then **I slipped**. The first screen says plainly that everything you earned stays, and offers **Log it and go on** or **Just reading**. Then **Are you safe right now?** with **I am safe**, **I need help now** and a **Safety facts** card. Then a few thoughts that often come after a slip, each with a reply. Then **Reach out**: one call or text. Then **Within a day**: **Slip Review now**, **Add it to my to-dos**, **Revisit my reasons**, **Step up support** or **Not now**. The slip goes into your log once. The only number that changes is the optional **current run**, and that one is off unless you turned it on. Your total days, skills, badges and Harbor stay exactly as they were.

## The practice drill

You can walk the craving flow with nothing at stake: from the last setup step tap **Practise SOS once**, or open **Settings → Help → SOS practice drill**, or tap it in the Explorer list. A banner says **Practice drill. Nothing goes in your log.** The reasons and comebacks are sample lines, and the end screen says **Drill done.** Nothing is saved except the fact that you did the drill.

> [!TIP]
> **Try it.** Run the drill from **Settings → Help**. Rate the craving, tap **Ride the wave**, step through SOBER with **Next**, tap **It helped**, then **Check in with the craving**, **It passed**, and **No**. Read the end screen and tap **Finish**. Now you know the way before you need it.

# 5. Learning

## The journey: eleven stops

**What you see.** The **Learn** tab opens on **Your journey**, with three small tabs: **Map**, **Plan** and **Glossary**. The map shows eleven stops on a dotted path. Each stop is a module with three to six short lessons, a place in the Harbor, and the tools it opens.

| Stop | Module | Where it lives | What it opens |
|---|---|---|---|
| M0 | Start Here | The harbor gate | The SOS drill, your first reasons, your pace |
| M1 | Understand Addiction | The Weather Station, ground floor | The signs, your risk factors, asking for help |
| M2 | Your Brain Can Heal | The Lighthouse, ground floor | Workbook, Roadmap plan view, Glossary |
| M3 | Find Your Why | The Compass Rose | Motivation Meter, Decisional Balance, Weekly Review |
| M4 | Set Yourself Up | The Pier | Support Network, Scripts, Quests |
| M5 | Become a Self-Expert | The Weather Station | Trigger Profile, Red Flags & Comebacks, Insights |
| M6 | Outsmart the Addicted Brain | The Lighthouse | Planner, Battle Plans, Talk back in SOS, Training drills |
| M7 | Be Present | The Cove | Guided practices |
| M8 | Rediscover Joy | The Garden | Activity Library, Rewards, Prediction Lab |
| M9 | Handle Hard Feelings | The Breakwater | Your own feeling tools in SOS |
| M10 | Your Recovery Plan | The Harbor Office | My Recovery Plan, Warning Sign Radar |

**What to tap.** Tap a stop. The order is a suggestion; nothing is locked, and you can start anywhere. The stop page lists its lessons with their minutes and a **Done** mark, and a **Checkpoint** button at the end.

**Tip.** Most lessons take three to six minutes.

## A lesson

**What you see.** A short set of screens with a progress bar: **A moment** (a few lines from one of five people in recovery, such as Meera or Raj), three or four cards, a **Quick check**, a **Try it** and a **Wrap up**. Each card has **Listen**, which reads it aloud in your device's own voice.

**What to tap.** **Next** and **Back**. On the Quick check, pick the answer that fits best: every answer shows why, the helpful reading is named softly, and nothing is marked wrong. On **Try it**, open the exercise or screen the lesson points to. On the Wrap up, read **What you built**, **Where it lives** and **One small step**; tap **Add to my to-dos** to keep the step, or **Why this works** for the research behind it. Then **Finish lesson**.

**What happens.** The lesson is marked done and adds 10 XP, the first time only. The last lesson of a stop leads to its **Checkpoint**: two quick questions to look back, with no pass mark. Finishing all the lessons of a stop opens the tools it unlocks.

## Exercises

An exercise is a short form, and what you write becomes a page in your Workbook. There are thirty-six, from **Three quick reasons** and **My triggers** to **Put it on trial**, **My activity menu**, **My warning signs** and **Learn from a slip**. Opened from a lesson, an exercise shows how one of the five people filled it in, plus hints. Opened from the Workbook, it shows your last answers so you can change them. **Finish later** saves a draft. A few exercises ask about hard things; they say so first and let you **Skip for now**. If you type words that sound like danger, a quiet line offers **Urgent help** without stopping you.

## The roadmap and the glossary

The **Plan** tab turns the eleven stops into weeks, based on your pace: **Gentle** (one stop every two weeks), **Steady** (one a week), **Focused** (two a week), **Custom**, or **Free** (no dates, just the order). A week that went by with its stop unfinished simply says **Still open**. After two weeks away, a **Welcome back** card offers **Re-plan from this week** or **Pick up where you left off**. The **Glossary** tab lists thirty-nine terms in plain words, with a search box.

> [!TIP]
> **Try it.** Open **Learn** and tap **Start Here**. Open the first lesson, **What this is, and what it is not**, and go through it with **Next**. On the Quick check, pick any answer and read the explanation. On the Wrap up, tap **Why this works**, then **Finish lesson**. Watch the stop page show one lesson done.

# 6. Tracking and insights

## Track

**What you see.** **+ Log** and **SOS** at the top, **Your days** if the counters are on, a line about Insights, and **Your log**: every entry, newest first, grouped by day, with **Show more** after thirty. Sample rows carry a **Sample** tag.

| Log | What it asks |
|---|---|
| Log a craving | Strength 0 to 10, what set it off, the thought, whether it was a red flag, what you tried and what helped, notes |
| Log a feeling | Which feeling, how strong, and the same chips and notes as a craving |
| Check-in | Morning: mood, worry, one thing you want from today. Evening: also peak craving, good moments, used today, sleep, meeting |
| Log an activity | Exercise, mindfulness, a meeting or something you enjoy; minutes; what it was; how much you enjoyed it |
| Note a slip | When, what was used, what was going on, notes |

**What to tap.** **+ Log**, a kind, what you can fill in, **Save**. The slip form opens with a kind note and a **Steps for after a slip** button that previews the SOS slip steps without saving anything. After you save a slip, the app says **It is in your log.** and offers **Next steps**.

**Tip.** Every form keeps your words if a save fails, and tells you why in plain language.

## Insights

**What you see.** Until you have seven logs (cravings, feelings and check-ins together), Track says **Insights appear after seven logs. You have N so far.** After that, **See your insights** opens a page of cards: **Craving strength by week** (a dot per week for eight weeks); **What set cravings off most** (your top five triggers); **When cravings came** (a grid of days and hours, darker where there were more, with a table view); **Skills that helped most**; **Mood and pleasant activities** (plain sentences from your evening check-ins); and **Your predictions** and **Your motivation over time** when you have them.

**What happens.** Under fourteen logs, every card carries a note that the sample is small, so read it lightly. The page says what it is: patterns to notice, not rules. The busy hours in the grid become the Planner's suggestions.

## What never goes down

The app keeps its numbers from a record of what you did that is only ever added to. So:

- **XP**, **skill levels**, **badges**, **ranks** and the **Harbor lights** only grow.
- **Your total days** count from your start date, minus the days you slipped. A slip is one day; it does not wipe the rest.
- The **current run** is a second counter that starts again after a slip. It is **off by default** (Settings → Look → **Show my current run**) and never shown in the progress panel.
- No points are ever given for claimed sober days. Counters count; they do not score.
- The **Warning Sign Radar** (early signs drawn from your own logs) is listed as a tool but has no screen in this edition. It is **coming**.

> [!TIP]
> **Try it.** With sample data loaded, open **Track** and tap **See your insights**. Read the small-sample note on a card, open **Show as a table** under the grid and find the busiest hour. Then open **Settings → Look** and switch **Show day counters** off and on to see how Track changes.

# 7. Planning

## The Actions tab

**What you see.** Seven cards, each with **Open**: **Planner**, **Battle Plans**, **Weekly Review**, **Quests**, **Prediction Lab**, **To-dos** and **Practices**.

## The day planner

**What you see.** One day at a time with **Previous day** and **Next day**, **Add a block**, **Your day**, **Open time** and, when it has ideas, **Ideas for open time**.

**What to tap.** **Add a block**: **What is it?**, the **Kind of block** (Should-do, Want-to, Recovery, Risky time), the day, start and end times, and, if you like, an activity and a small reward. Tick **This could be a risky time** for a block that might be hard. **Save block**.

**What happens.** The block appears in **Your day**. **Open time** lists every stretch of two hours or more between 7 am and 11 pm with **Add here**, because empty evenings are where cravings like to live. **Ideas for open time** suggests want-tos from your Activity Library at your riskiest hours, with **Plan this**. A risky block shows **Add a Battle Plan**. When a block's time has passed it asks **Not marked yet. How did it go?** with **Done** and **Skipped**, in the same calm colours as everything else; nothing is ever "overdue".

## Battle Plans and the calendar file

**What you see.** Your plans under **Coming up** and **Looking back**, with **New Battle Plan** at the top.

**What to tap.** **New Battle Plan**: **What is the time or place?**, the day and times, **What makes it risky?**, **What I will do**, **Backups** (one per line, up to ten), **Someone I can reach** (from your Support Network) and **How I will leave if I need to**. **Save the plan**.

**What happens.** The plan becomes a risky block in your Planner, and Today shows it under **Your Battle Plan** on that day. **Add to calendar** saves a small calendar file (`.ics`) to your device, which your phone's calendar app can open; the buddy's name is left out of it on purpose. When the time has passed, the plan asks **Did the plan show up when it counted?** with **Yes, it did** and **Not this time**. Both answers are thanked and earn the same small credit.

## The Weekly Review

**What you see.** **Start the review**, then five screens with **Back** and **Next**: **The last seven days** (highlights from your log); **The forecast check** (the risky times you planned beside the cravings and feelings you logged that day, just to notice); **Where you are now** (your stage, a 0 to 10, and **What would move it up by one?**); **The week ahead** (risky times coming up, each with **Add a Battle Plan**); and **Want-tos and a focus** (up to three things you want to do this week, each with a small reward, and one skill to practise).

**What happens.** **Save the review** writes the want-tos into your Planner, saves the review once for the week and adds 25 XP. **Add a weekly reminder** saves a calendar file that repeats at 6 pm on your review day. Nothing here is scored.

## Quests, to-dos and the Prediction Lab

**Quests** are eight short series of real-life actions, such as **Clear the Space**, **Build Your Crew**, **SOBER Training**, **Everyday Mindfulness** and the **7-Day Activity Experiment**. Some steps tick themselves from what you log; the others have **Mark done**. A finished quest names its badge. There is no timer, no loss and no random reward.

**To-dos** is a short list: **Add a to-do**, an optional day, **Done**, **Go** when a to-do points to a screen, and **Remove**. A past day looks like any other; nothing is late.

**Prediction Lab**: write **What will you do?**, guess how much you will enjoy it from 0 to 10, **Save the prediction**. Afterwards tap **How was it?** and rate it. The result reads in words: **You expected 3; it was 6. It went better than you guessed.** Moods often guess low; this is how you find out.

## Practices

Eight guided practices with their length: **Breathing with awareness** (5 to 30 minutes, your choice), **Body scan** (10), **SOBER breathing** (2), **Urge surfing** (10), **Mindful shower**, **Mindful eating** and **Mindful walking** (5 each), and **Give it a beat** (2). Tap **Start**, then **Begin**. The lines appear one at a time with a timer; **Read aloud** uses your device's own voice; **Pause**, **Back a line** and **Next line** are always there. **Finish** opens **How was that?** with a note and **Did it help?**, then **Save and finish**.

**Coming:** the **Rewards** page (the real-world rewards you chose) is listed among the tools but has no screen yet; it says so when you open it.

> [!TIP]
> **Try it.** Open **Actions → Planner**. Tap **Add here** on tonight's open stretch, write a want-to and save. Then open **Battle Plans → New Battle Plan**, write one line under **What I will do**, save it, and tap **Add to calendar**. Tomorrow, come back and answer **Did the plan show up when it counted?**

# 8. Your workbook

## The browser

**What you see.** **My Recovery Plan** at the top, then four groups of pages: **My Why** (Reasons Card, Pros and cons, How ready I feel, Signs I noticed), **Know Myself** (My triggers, Red-flag thoughts, Thought Journal, The spiral, My craving signature, My anger signature, My warning signs), **My People** (My Support Network, What I will say) and **My Joys** (Activity Library, Should and want, Distraction deck). Each card says when it was last saved. **Show sensitive items** at the bottom reveals one more page, **Risk factors**, private and only for you.

**What to tap.** A card opens the page: your latest answers as text, **Change it** (or **Fill it in** when empty), a **History** of earlier versions, **Print**, and **Practise with this** when a screen uses the page (for example, **Red-flag thoughts** opens the Spot the Red Flag game).

## The Reasons Card

**What you see.** Your reasons in up to four groups: **My reasons**, **What using costs me**, **What changing gives me** and **What would be different**. The first comes from your three quick reasons; the others fill in as you do the **Both sides of both choices** and **Four lenses** exercises.

**What to tap.** **Big view** shows the card in large type with **Call someone** underneath; this is what SOS opens. **Save as a lock-screen image** makes a picture of your card on your phone. **My people** jumps to your Support Network.

## The Support Network

# 10. Settings and your data

Tap the settings icon in the top bar. Settings has seven tabs: **Privacy**, **Look**, **Rhythm**, **Names**, **Help**, **About** and **Your data**.

## Lock and PIN

**What you see.** **Privacy** starts with one line about where your notebook is kept. Without a PIN it offers **Set a PIN**. With one it shows **Lock the notebook when I am away** (after 1, 5 or 15 minutes, or never), **Lock now**, **Change PIN** and **Remove PIN**.

**What to tap.** **Set a PIN**: four to twelve digits, twice, the auto-lock time, **Set PIN**. Changing the auto-lock or removing the PIN asks for your PIN first.

**What happens.** With a PIN, every row of your notebook is encrypted on the device and only opens with it. Leaving the phone alone for the time you chose locks the app; the lock screen keeps **Urgent help** and **Ride the wave (SOBER)** open without the PIN. A wrong PIN gets a short message and a one-second pause. If the lock time passes while you are inside SOS, SOS stays open and only your own notes hide.

**Discreet mode** is on the same tab. The app shows **Notebook** as its name, the SOS button says **Help**, the Harbor and the day counters are hidden, and your Reasons Card is titled **Notes**.

## Look, Rhythm, Names

**Look**: the theme (same as my device, light or dark; the moon button in the top bar also switches), text size (normal, large, larger), **Calm mode** (fewer colours, no celebrations, a quieter Today), **High contrast**, **Reduce motion**, **Show day counters** and **Show my current run**. **Rhythm**: your check-in times (leave one empty to skip it), your review day and your learning pace. **Names**: your name, your guide's name (the companion who greets you on Today), and the words the app uses for your days.

## Help and About

**Help** has **Replay the tour**, the training drills, a list of **How do I...?** questions with **Take me there**, and the **Show tips** and **Show all features now** switches. **About** has the early-access notice, what the app is and is not, an **Urgent help** button, the version, and the **Evidence** list: thirty-two sources behind the techniques.

## Export and import a backup

**What you see.** **Your data** starts with **Backup**: **Last backup: never** (or the date), **Save a backup file** and **Remind me to save a backup**. Then **Bring back a backup** with **Choose a backup file**, then **Print**, **Sample data** and **Erase everything**.

**What to tap.** **Save a backup file**; type your PIN if you have one. A file named like `1-addic-2026-11-08.json` is saved to your device. Keep it somewhere private, such as your own cloud drive or a memory card. To bring a backup back, tap **Choose a backup file**, pick it, and confirm **Replace with the backup**.

**What happens.** The backup holds your whole notebook as readable text. **It is not locked by your PIN**, so anyone who has the file can read it. Bringing a backup back replaces everything on this device; your PIN stays. The app then says how many logs, answers and contacts came back. Today reminds you when a backup is two weeks overdue.

## Erase and sample data

**Erase everything** removes every entry, every setting and the PIN from this device, after you confirm. It cannot be undone, so save a backup first if you may want it back. Afterwards **Start again** takes you to setup. **Load sample data** fills a fresh notebook with marked sample entries; it is greyed out once you have your own profile. **Erase sample data** removes only the rows marked Sample.

## If something looks wrong

| What you see | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Not saved — export before closing | The browser is blocking storage, often a private window | Save a backup now, then open the file in a normal window and bring it back |
| My data is gone | A different browser, folder or window, or the browser's data was cleared | Open the file from the same place in the same browser, or bring back your latest backup |
| I forgot my PIN | It cannot be recovered | On the lock screen open **Forgot your PIN?**, erase, then bring back a backup |
| Read-aloud says there is no voice | Your device has no local voice | Read the text on screen; the app never uses a network voice |
| A page says it is coming in a later version | That part is not built yet | Nothing is broken; the rest works |

> [!TIP]

# 11. What the app never does

- **It never uses the internet.** The file tells the browser to refuse every network request. There is no sync, no cloud and no update check. The only web links are the meeting finders and the evidence list, and they say they need internet.
- **It never tracks you.** No analytics, no ads, no account, no sign-in.
- **It never sends your contacts anywhere.** A phone number becomes a Call button and nothing else. Even the calendar file for a Battle Plan leaves your buddy's name out.
- **It never gates SOS.** No PIN, tip, tour, lesson or lock screen stands between you and help.
- **It never gives points for claimed sober days.** Counters count; XP comes only from things you did, such as a log, a lesson or a practice.
- **It never takes anything away after a slip.** XP, skills, badges, ranks, total days and the Harbor only grow. Only the optional current run starts again, and it is off unless you switch it on.
- **It never uses random rewards.** No spinners, no lucky prizes, no timers that punish you, no countdowns on badges.
- **It never shames.** A slip is one day in the log. A past plan is never "overdue". A missed week is "still open".
- **It never speaks through the network.** Read-aloud uses only a voice that lives on your device.
- **It never quotes the book it is based on.** Every word in the app was written for the app; the ideas are credited in the Evidence list.
