# ADR 0008: Substance scope — alcohol and drugs; nicotine later; behavioural out for now

- **Decision:** D8 (PLAN §15) · **Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 16 October 2026

## Context

The book covers alcohol and drugs. The owner wants to keep that scope now and leave the door open.

## Decision

Alcohol and drugs. Nicotine as an optional extra later. Behavioural addictions out for now; they, and a cut-down mode for people who want to regulate, could be added later as separate, dedicated modes.

## Consequences

- Onboarding offers alcohol and drug substances now.
- Tobacco and gutka are common in Gujarat (ADR 0010), so nicotine is likely the first extra to consider after the friends release.
- Any later mode gets its own content, evidence and review; the plan's non-goals note the possibility.
