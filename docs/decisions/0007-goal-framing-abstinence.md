# ADR 0007: Goal framing — abstinence-focused, with a contemplation path

- **Decision:** D7 (PLAN §15) · **Status:** Accepted (default) · **Date:** 16 October 2026

## Context

The source workbook and its evidence are abstinence-based. Allowing "cut down" goals would change the lessons, counters and safety messages.

## Decision

Abstinence-focused, with a contemplation path for people who are not sure yet. Safety information (withdrawal, overdose, crisis) for everyone regardless of goal.

## Consequences

- Onboarding, counters and messages assume a stop goal.
- Under D8 the owner noted that a dedicated "cut down" or regulation mode could be added later as a separate mode. It is out of scope now; if added, it needs its own evidence base, content and clinical review.
