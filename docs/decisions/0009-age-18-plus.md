# ADR 0009: Age — adults only

- **Decision:** D9 (PLAN §15) · **Status:** Accepted (default) · **Date:** 16 October 2026

## Decision

The app is for people aged 18 and over, stated in onboarding, the privacy policy and the store rating.

## Consequences

- No design, consent or legal work for minors.
- Store age ratings are set accordingly at Step 6.3.
