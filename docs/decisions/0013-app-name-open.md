# ADR 0013: App name — open, with a shortlist

- **Decision:** D13 (PLAN §15) · **Status:** **Open** · **Date:** 16 October 2026

## Context

The owner likes "1-addic" and is open to variations ("One-Addic", "Addic-one") or something else. Constraints from the plan: never the book's title; discreet, because the name shows on the home screen, in notifications and on store receipts; a trademark and store-name check before use. On iPhone the installed name cannot change at runtime, so the store name is what people see.

## Shortlist

| Name | Meaning | Discreet? | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Kinaro** (કિનારો) | shore, coast | Yes | Fits the Harbor world; pleasant in English; no store collision found in a quick search |
| **Laher** (લહેર) | a wave; also a relaxed, happy mood in everyday Gujarati | Yes | Ties to urge surfing; warm local connotation; no collision found |
| **Ek Din** (એક દિવસ / एक दिन) | "one day", as in one day at a time | Yes | Keeps the owner's "1"; widely understood across India; check for film or media collisions |
| Hodi (હોડી) | a small boat | Yes | Harbor fit; short |
| Divo (દીવો) | a lamp, a small light | Yes | The lighthouse's light; short |
| Nav (નાવ) | boat; also "nav" as in navigation | Yes | Bilingual meaning; very generic for trademark purposes |
| Harbor | the game world | Yes | Common word; likely collisions |
| One-Addic, Addic-One, UnAddic | plays on the working title | No | Reveal the purpose to anyone who sees the phone or a receipt |

## Recommendation

Kinaro, Laher or Ek Din, in that order, with "1-addic" kept as the project's codename and folder name. Run a trademark search and a store-name check before the friends release; the quick web checks found no obvious app-store collisions for the top three, but they are not a legal search.

## Consequences

- Step 0.5 proceeds with a placeholder name; the design system must not bake the name into assets.
- The decision is recorded in a superseding ADR when the owner chooses.
